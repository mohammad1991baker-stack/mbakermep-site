Case #04

Where Did the “80 cm Rule” for Concealed Spaces Come From?

Eng. Mohammad Yacoub Baker · Fire protection design notes

And does a ceiling void ≤ 80 cm always mean that sprinklers are not required?

Not necessarily.

Under BS EN 12845 – Clause 5.4:

> 0.8 m → Sprinkler protection is required.
≤ 0.8 m → Sprinklers are still required if the space contains combustible materials or is constructed with combustible materials.

But NFPA 13 follows a different approach.

NFPA 13:2019 does not establish a general 80 cm rule. Section 9.2.1 lists specific conditions where sprinklers may be omitted from concealed spaces—for example, certain noncombustible or limited-combustible spaces with minimal combustible loading and restricted access, as well as several specific construction arrangements.

So under NFPA, you cannot simply measure the void and say:

“It is 70 cm, so no sprinklers are required.”

The construction, combustible loading, access, and the applicable NFPA omission condition must be checked.

The 80 cm rule is not a universal rule. Always check the code behind the number.

These notes are for design discussion. Always apply the code edition adopted for your project and confirm with the AHJ.
