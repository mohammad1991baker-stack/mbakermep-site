Case #05

The 1.2 m Canopy Rule

Eng. Mohammad Yacoub Baker · Fire protection design notes

Does every canopy or exterior projection greater than 1.2 m require sprinklers?

Not always.

According to NFPA 13:2019 – Section 9.2.3, exterior projections extending more than 4 ft (1.2 m) generally require sprinkler protection.

However, NFPA 13 provides several exceptions depending on the construction materials and configuration.

One important detail is often missed:

If combustibles are stored beneath the projection, sprinklers are required when the projection exceeds only 2 ft (600 mm).

So before deciding based on 1.2 m, check the complete conditions and exceptions.

The number alone doesn’t give you the answer.

Reference: NFPA 13:2019 – §9.2.3

These notes are for design discussion. Always apply the code edition adopted for your project and confirm with the AHJ.
