Same Temperature Rating, Different Response Time

Eng. Mohammad Yacoub Baker · Design notesم. محمد يعقوب بكر · ملاحظات تصميم (المقال باللغة الإنجليزية)

Two sprinklers rated at 68°C (155°F) can operate at different times under the same hot-gas conditions.

The difference is thermal sensitivity, measured by the Response Time Index (RTI). A lower RTI means faster thermal response. The temperature rating tells you at what temperature the element operates, not how quickly it heats up.

NFPA 13 classifies thermal elements as:
Fast response: RTI of 50 (m·s)½ or less
Standard response: RTI of 80 (m·s)½ or more

Why it matters in design: NFPA 13 requires quick-response sprinklers in light hazard occupancies (with limited exceptions), and their use can permit a reduction in the design area under specific conditions.

Always verify both the temperature rating and the response classification against the design criteria and the sprinkler listing.

Reference: NFPA 13 (2025), Chapter 3 Definitions
