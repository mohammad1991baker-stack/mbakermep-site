Case #08

Fire Hose Cabinet (FHC)

Eng. Mohammad Yacoub Baker · Fire protection design notes

A Fire Hose Cabinet doesn’t always need a separate piping network.

NFPA 13:2019 permits certain 1½-inch (40 mm) hose connections to be supplied directly from the sprinkler system, subject to specific conditions.

According to Sections 16.15.1.3 and 16.15.1.4:

  • Wet Pipe System: Connections for fire purposes only are permitted on wet sprinkler systems.
  • Connection Location: Permitted arrangements include sprinkler risers upstream of sprinkler control valves and, under specific conditions, ceiling sprinkler piping.
  • Minimum Pipe Size: The sprinkler pipe at the connection point must be at least 2½ inches (65 mm), with an exception for qualifying looped/grid systems.
  • FHC Supply Branch: The branch pipe size is determined separately under the applicable code requirements.
  • Pressure Regulation: Pressure-regulating devices must be provided where required.

Important distinction: The 65 mm requirement refers to the sprinkler pipe at the connection point, not the 40 mm hose connection inside the FHC.

A separate network isn’t always necessary. Understanding the permitted connection arrangements can simplify piping layouts and reduce installation costs.

Reference: NFPA 13:2019 — Sections 16.15.1.3 & 16.15.1.4.

These notes are for design discussion. Always apply the code edition adopted for your project and confirm with the AHJ.
