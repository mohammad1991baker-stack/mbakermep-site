Case #15

ESFR Protection — Applications & Limitations

Eng. Mohammad Yacoub Baker · Fire protection design notes

ESFR can provide ceiling-only protection in many warehouses, provided the applicable storage and installation criteria are met.

Key restrictions:

Solid-shelf racks require in-rack protection in accordance with Chapter 25.
2019: §23.1.4.1 | 2025: §23.1.3.1

Open-top containers are not permitted.
2019: §23.1.4.2 | 2025: §23.1.3.2

Minimum operating pressure must match the commodity, storage height and building height.
2019: §23.1.4.3 | 2025: §23.1.3.3

These conditions remain in the 2025 edition.

For hydraulic design, 2025 §23.2.2 specifies 12 hydraulically most demanding sprinklers—four on each of three branch lines—unless otherwise specified.

ESFR selection starts with the storage arrangement, not just the K-factor.

Reference: NFPA 13 (2019 & 2025), Chapter 23.

These notes are for design discussion. Always apply the code edition adopted for your project and confirm with the AHJ.
