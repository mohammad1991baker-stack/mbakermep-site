Case #23

Same Pump. Different Start Setting.

Eng. Mohammad Yacoub Baker · Fire protection design notes

Copying pressure settings from an older project can overlook a change in Annex guidance.

NFPA 20 recommends starting the main fire pump below the jockey pump’s start pressure by:

2019 edition: 5 psi.
2025 edition: 10 psi.

Worked example: assume the jockey pump starts at 140 psi.

2019 guidance: 140 − 5 = 135 psi.
2025 guidance: 140 − 10 = 130 psi.

These are illustrative values, not universal setpoints. Verify the adopted edition, system conditions, and controller capabilities.

Which edition are your project’s settings based on?

Reference: NFPA 20 (2019 and 2025), Annex Section A.14.2.6(4). Annex guidance is explanatory, not mandatory.

These notes are for design discussion. Always apply the code edition adopted for your project and confirm with the AHJ.
