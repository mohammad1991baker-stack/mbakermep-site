Case #28

ESFR: Area, Spacing and the 12-Sprinkler Rule

Eng. Mohammad Yacoub Baker · Fire protection design notes

A warehouse may have hundreds of ESFR sprinklers. That does not mean all of them enter the hydraulic calculation.

Three checks matter:

1. Coverage per sprinkler

The general maximum is 100 ft² (9.3 m²), and the minimum is 64 ft² (5.9 m²), subject to permitted exceptions.

Coverage and spacing must both comply. A permitted spacing of 12 ft does not make a 12 × 12 ft layout acceptable: that would cover 144 ft² per sprinkler.

2. Spacing

The general minimum spacing is 8 ft (2.4 m).

For storage up to 25 ft with a ceiling up to 30 ft, the maximum spacing is 12 ft (3.7 m).

For ceilings above 30 ft, Table 14.2.8.2.1 limits maximum spacing to 10 ft (3.0 m), subject to permitted exceptions.

Read the spacing clause together with its table.

3. Hydraulic calculation

The usual design includes the 12 most hydraulically demanding sprinklers: four on each of three branch lines, unless another provision requires a different number.

Illustrative example:
At 3 × 3 m spacing, each sprinkler covers 9 m². The 12-sprinkler group covers 108 m².

However, ESFR demand is not calculated by multiplying that area by an assumed density.

Coverage determines the layout. The storage hazard and protection criteria determine the required discharge.

Reference: NFPA 13 (2019), Sections 14.2.8, 14.2.9 and 27.2.4.4; Table 14.2.8.2.1. Apply the edition adopted for the project.
