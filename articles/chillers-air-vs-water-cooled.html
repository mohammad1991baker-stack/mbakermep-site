Air-Cooled vs Water-Cooled Chillers: What's the Full Picture?

Eng. Mohammad Yacoub Baker · Design notesم. محمد يعقوب بكر · ملاحظات تصميم (المقال باللغة الإنجليزية)

The attached 5,000 TR worked example compares:

  • Electricity and cooling-tower water consumption
  • Water treatment and annual operating costs
  • Unit capacities and N+1 redundancy
  • Equipment space and maintenance clearances
  • Historical capital-cost benchmarks and payback sensitivity

Under the stated assumptions, the water-cooled option saves approximately 42% in system electricity and 14% in annual operating costs within the comparison boundary.

Calculations, assumptions, real photos and references are included in the full 23-page document attached to the original LinkedIn post.

These notes are for design discussion. Check them against the codes, data and conditions of your own project.
