Case #18

A 1000 GPM Fire Pump — Can It Meet a 1400 GPM Demand?

Eng. Mohammad Yacoub Baker · Fire protection design notes

An increased hydraulic demand does not automatically mean replacing the fire pump.

Consider:
• Existing pump: 1000 GPM @ 100 psi.
• New demand at the pump discharge: 1400 GPM @ 80 psi.

NFPA 20 (2025), Section 4.10.1, permits the greatest single system demand to be up to 150% of the pump’s rated flow.

However, 1400 GPM being below that limit does not establish compliance.

Verify the actual pump curve, available suction pressure, driver capacity, and applicable acceptance requirements.

The nameplate alone cannot confirm whether the pump meets the new duty point.

Would you approve the existing pump based on these two data points alone?

Reference: NFPA 20 (2025), Section 4.10.1.
