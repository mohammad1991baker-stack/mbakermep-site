Open Stair / Escalator: Do We Really Need Deluge?

Eng. Mohammad Yacoub Baker · Design notesم. محمد يعقوب بكر · ملاحظات تصميم (المقال باللغة الإنجليزية)

In one of our mall projects, we had an open vertical opening around a stair/escalator, and the initial fire protection requirement was to provide a:

Deluge-Type Water Curtain.

Before proceeding with the design, we went back to NFPA 13 to understand the available protection approach.

And this is where it became interesting.

For vertical openings where sprinkler protection is permitted as an alternative to enclosure, NFPA 13 9.3.5 provides:

Draft Curtain + Closely Spaced Sprinklers

with the following arrangement:

  • Draft curtain immediately adjacent to the opening
  • Minimum depth: 450 mm (18 in.)
  • Sprinkler spacing: maximum 1.8 m (6 ft)
  • Sprinklers located 150–300 mm (6–12 in.) from the draft curtain

But the most interesting part was in the NFPA 13 Handbook commentary:

The sprinklers used for this protection can be either open type or automatic type, and the Handbook notes that the use of deluge-type water curtains has become quite rare since the early 1960s.

Why is this important?

Because choosing between open-type Deluge and automatic sprinklers can significantly change the hydraulic design and system arrangement.

NFPA 13:2025 and 19.3.3 requires a water curtain discharge of 37 L/min per linear meter, with no sprinkler discharging less than 57 L/min, and provides separate calculation provisions when automatic sprinklers are used.

So before automatically specifying a Deluge system for an open stair or escalator, the real engineering question should be:

“Is Deluge actually required for this opening, or is the NFPA 13 Draft Curtain + Automatic Sprinkler arrangement permitted?”

That question can affect the hydraulic demand, fire pump, water tank, piping, controls, coordination and cost.

References
NFPA 13 (2022) — 9.3.5 Vertical Openings & Figure A.9.3.5
NFPA 13 Automatic Sprinkler Systems Handbook (2022) — 9.3.5 Commentary
NFPA 13 (2025) — 19.3.3 Water Curtains

These notes are for design discussion. Always apply the code edition adopted for your project and confirm with the AHJ.
